Protection Order Petition: Practice and Completeness Check

Practice filling in Indiana's Petition for an Order for Protection (PO-0100) and Confidential Form (PO-0104), see what is missing, and download a worksheet to copy onto the official forms.

    Based on the official forms PO-0100 (rev. 07/2026), PO-0104 (rev. 07/2019) and instructions PO-0102 (rev. 05/2025) from the Indiana Office of Court Services, and the Protection Order Deskbook. Forms change; always use the current official version to file. Everything you enter stays in this browser tab and is never saved or sent.
